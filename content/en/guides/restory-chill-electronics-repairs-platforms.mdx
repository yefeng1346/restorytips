## What Playable does and does not say

Playable is a compatibility label, not a detailed performance report. It does not confirm stable frame rate, every repair interaction, native button mapping, vibration, or whether mouse input is needed for a particular workbench action. The official store also does not publish a complete controller-support statement or button layout.

For Steam Deck, keep platform availability and input quality in separate fields. The game can be listed as Playable based on the official launch statement. This avoids turning a single compatibility label into a broader hardware promise.

## Windows and Mac notes

Windows players should use the official minimum table. The supplied Windows minimum is Win10 x64, a 2-core/4-thread CPU, 4 GB RAM, GTX 750ti, DirectX 11, and 1 GB available storage.

Mac players need macOS 14 or newer, an Apple M1 processor, 8 GB RAM, Apple integrated GPU on M1, and 1 GB available storage according to the official minimum table. The store also requires an Apple processor.
