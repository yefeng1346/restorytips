## What Playable does and does not say

Playable is a compatibility label, not a detailed performance report. It does not confirm stable frame rate, every repair interaction, native button mapping, vibration, or whether mouse input is needed for a particular workbench action. The official store also does not publish a complete controller-support statement or button layout.

For Steam Deck, keep platform availability and input quality in separate fields.
