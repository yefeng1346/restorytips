In ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs, “Internet Plan” refers to the in-game Y2K-era browser and online-order system, not a real-world broadband subscription. The browser helps you hunt for spare parts, and online or email requests add jobs to the repair shop.

## The browser is part of the repair economy

The official Steam description connects the browser to spare-part research. It is a period-styled web tool inside the shop, used while managing customer work, money, and device repairs.

Use the browser as a research step after you understand the current order. Note the device category, the faulty part, and the spare you are looking for before treating a listing as the right answer.

## Email orders and confirmed milestones

The achievements page gives three useful Internet milestones. Master of the Internet is tied to accepting the first email order, WWW to completing 25 email orders, and Internet Business to completing 100 email orders. These conditions confirm that email work is trackable, but they do not prove that every online request uses the same interface or reward.

Keep a count of accepted and completed email orders separately if the game displays both values.

## Online requests and spare parts

Online requests belong beside customer work rather than replacing the repair loop. They provide another way to receive a job or find a supply, while the workbench sequence remains disassembly, cleaning, faulty-part replacement, reassembly, and delivery.

For a practical note, record the request type, device family, required part, source, and completion state. This links the Internet page to the Parts Catalog and Walkthrough.
