## Where cleaning fits in a repair

Cleaning is not an optional decoration step in the published repair description. It sits between disassembly and faulty-part replacement, so a repair should be treated as a complete sequence rather than a quick part swap. ## Cleaning achievements

The official Steam achievements provide the clearest confirmed cleaning targets. Clean Job is tied to cleaning the workspace. Big cleaning! requires cleaning 100 parts, while Galactic cleaning! requires cleaning 1,000 parts. These are cumulative goals from the public achievement conditions; the page does not provide a guaranteed fastest route or a fixed number of parts per order.

Hands Free is also connected to cleaning because its public condition is buying the automatic ultrasonic bath. That achievement confirms the bath belongs to the formal cleaning system, but it does not establish the exact purchase cost, shop level, or unlock date. Record those values from the full game only after checking the current release build.

For a natural route, clean every part that belongs to a real customer order and keep a separate count for the cumulative goals. Do not treat the 100-part and 1,000-part targets as proof that a specific device contains a known number of cleanable pieces. The complete device roster and per-order part counts remain **待确认**.

## Ultrasonic bath and sonic-bath reports

The official update material has recorded a known issue in which some gadgets could become stuck in the sonic bath. This tells us that the bath is a meaningful repair tool and also a place where a player may need troubleshooting. It does not prove that every stuck-device report has the same cause or that one workaround is guaranteed on the current build.

An older Steam Community discussion reported a workaround for a case where the bath made a sound but the dirt did not disappear: drag the dirty part to the cleaning container at the top right of the table, then choose the brush interaction. This is a community report rather than an official universal instruction, so label it **待确认** and re-test it before publishing it as a fixed step.

## A practical troubleshooting record

When cleaning does not register, first note the device, part, tool, and current repair stage. Then record whether the part is visibly dirty, whether the bath produces a sound, and whether the gadget is trapped in the bath. This evidence keeps a single community report from being mistaken for a general rule.

If the issue persists, compare the current build with the official announcements and the Steam Community technical discussions. Avoid promising that restarting, changing a setting, or moving the camera fixes every case because those remedies were not confirmed in the supplied material. The safest published status for unresolved behavior is **待确认**.

After the part is clean, return to the normal loop: replace the faulty component, reassemble the device, and complete the order. Cleaning achievements can be tracked alongside ordinary jobs, but the research does not confirm whether every cleaning action counts in the same way across Demo and full release. Keep that version comparison **待确认**.
