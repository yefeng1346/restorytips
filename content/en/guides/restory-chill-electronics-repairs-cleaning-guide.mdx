The ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs cleaning guide is simple at the system level: disassemble the device, clean the separated parts, replace faulty components, and reassemble the order. Cleaning is part of the main repair loop, and the official achievement page confirms milestones for cleaning the workspace, 100 parts, and 1,000 parts.

## Where cleaning fits in a repair

Cleaning is not an optional decoration step in the published repair description. It sits between disassembly and faulty-part replacement, so a repair should be treated as a complete sequence rather than a quick part swap.

<RepairStep number="01" title="Separate the device first">
  Begin by taking the customer device apart. Keep the parts associated with the current order, because the official loop describes cleaning after disassembly and before the final reassembly.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="02" title="Clean the exposed parts">
  Work through the dirty components with the available cleaning system. Cleaning is a core activity in the published repair loop.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="03" title="Replace the faulty component">
  Once the cleaning stage is complete, replace the faulty part with the appropriate spare.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="04" title="Reassemble and deliver">
  Rebuild the device and finish the customer order. If a component still appears dirty or an interaction has not completed, check the current build before assuming that the repair sequence is wrong.
</RepairStep>

## Cleaning achievements

The official Steam achievements provide the clearest cleaning targets. Clean Job is tied to cleaning the workspace. Big cleaning! requires cleaning 100 parts, while Galactic cleaning! requires cleaning 1,000 parts. These are cumulative goals from the public achievement conditions.

Hands Free is also connected to cleaning because its public condition is buying the automatic ultrasonic bath. That achievement confirms the bath belongs to the formal cleaning system.

For a natural route, clean every part that belongs to a real customer order and keep a separate count for the cumulative goals.

## Ultrasonic bath and sonic-bath reports

The official update material has recorded a known issue in which some gadgets could become stuck in the sonic bath. This tells us that the bath is a meaningful repair tool and also a place where a player may need troubleshooting. It does not prove that every stuck-device report has the same cause or that one workaround is guaranteed on the current build.

Steam Community discussions include reports about gadgets becoming stuck in the sonic bath. Use the official update feed and current community discussions when troubleshooting a specific build.

## A practical troubleshooting record

When cleaning does not register, first note the device, part, tool, and current repair stage. Then record whether the part is visibly dirty, whether the bath produces a sound, and whether the gadget is trapped in the bath. This evidence keeps a single community report from being mistaken for a general rule.

If the issue persists, compare the current build with the official announcements and the Steam Community technical discussions. Record the device, part, tool, and repair stage before reporting the issue.

After the part is clean, return to the normal loop: replace the faulty component, reassemble the device, and complete the order. Cleaning achievements can be tracked alongside ordinary jobs.
