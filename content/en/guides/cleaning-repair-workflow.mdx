The official description gives ReStory's repair loop in a clear order. Use this page as the safe overview until every interaction has been checked against the full release.

<RepairStep number="01" title="Disassemble">
  Take the nostalgic device apart so its faulty components can be inspected.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="02" title="Clean">
  Clean the device and its components with the available shop tools. The precise tool names, timing, and interaction order are **待确认**.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="03" title="Replace faulty parts">
  Research and source spare parts through the Y2K-era browser. The exact stock and part combinations are **待确认**.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="04" title="Reassemble">
  Put the device back together, then continue the customer order and shop-management loop.
</RepairStep>
