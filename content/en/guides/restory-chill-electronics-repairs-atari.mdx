ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs includes officially licensed Atari consoles. The official notes cover Atari 2600 and 2800, the CX40 joystick, and an in-game teardown with four screws, shells, a motherboard, a red button, a joystick, and power cables. This page describes the game’s simplified repair interaction rather than real hardware repair.

## Why Atari appears in ReStory

The Steam store identifies officially licensed Atari consoles as part of the game’s nostalgic hardware range. A tinyBuild announcement explains the Atari collaboration and the Japanese-market background of Atari 2600 and 2800. That context fits the game’s mid-2000s Tokyo setting and its focus on restoring recognizable electronics.

The collaboration is more specific than a generic console reference: Atari 2600/2800 and the CX40 joystick are named in the official material.

## Atari 2600 and CX40 teardown notes

The official teardown description gives a compact in-game parts picture. It mentions four screws, the motherboard, the joystick, the top and bottom shell, a red button, and power cables. The game simplifies the connection between the motherboard and power cables so the task remains a playable repair activity.
