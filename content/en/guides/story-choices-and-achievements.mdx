# Choices, Endings & Achievements

ReStory is not only a repair checklist. Customer conversations and decisions can affect the lives around the shop, and the official description presents the story as branching with multiple endings.

## Confirmed progression signals

- The story is advertised as 15+ hours for the main route.
- The full game includes 50 Steam Achievements.
- Customer decisions can affect the shop and the stories you see.
- The exact number of endings and hidden story conditions are **待确认**.

<Callout title="Achievement-safe publishing" tone="gold">
  Use the official Steam achievement list for public conditions. Hidden achievement descriptions and route requirements should be checked in-game before they are presented as a complete walkthrough.
</Callout>

## Suggested route notes

When documenting a choice, record the customer context, the visible options, the immediate result, and the later story effect. Do not label one route as the only correct answer unless the game explicitly makes that choice irreversible or required.
