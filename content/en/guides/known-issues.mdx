# Known Issues & Launch Notes

The official Steam Community announcements reported several launch-build issues. This page separates those reports from details that require a later verification pass.

## Reported launch issues

- The `How Did I Do It` and `Frugal` achievements could unlock early.
- A firmware issue affected Guitar Legend and MyPod.
- Inventory could be unavailable in competition mode.
- An IDM device disassembly error was reported.
- The mail tab notification could remain visible incorrectly.
- Ultrawide screens had disassembly bugs.
- Gadgets could become stuck in the sonic bath.

<Callout title="Patch status" tone="teal">
  The live status of each issue can change with post-launch fixes. Check the latest official announcement before turning this list into a permanent troubleshooting guarantee.
</Callout>
