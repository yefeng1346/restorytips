# ReStory Beginner Guide

ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs puts you behind a small electronics repair shop in mid-2000s Tokyo. This first-hour guide follows the confirmed shop loop and keeps details that still need an in-game check clearly marked.

## Understand the shop loop

<RepairStep number="01" title="Accept the customer work">
  Customer conversations and repair requests are the starting point for each job. The choices you make can affect the shop and the stories around it.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="02" title="Take the device apart">
  The official game description confirms a repair sequence built around disassembly, cleaning, faulty-part replacement, and reassembly.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="03" title="Clean and replace faulty parts">
  Use the shop tools and research supplies through the Y2K-era web browser. The exact item-by-item part catalog is **Unconfirmed** until it is checked in the full game.
</RepairStep>

<RepairStep number="04" title="Reassemble and manage the shop">
  Rebuild the device, manage money, and keep an eye on the customer story that follows. The main story is advertised as 15+ hours and is replayable because of its branching structure.
</RepairStep>

<Callout title="What is confirmed" tone="teal">
  The game is a cozy narrative-driven shop management simulation by Mandragora and tinyBuild. It is available on Steam for Windows and macOS, and Steam lists the full game as Playable on Steam Deck.
</Callout>

## Story choices and progress

ReStory has customer conversations, choices that affect lives and the shop, and a branching storyline with multiple endings. The exact number and conditions of endings are **Unconfirmed**; do not treat an unverified route list as a complete answer.

The full game has 50 Steam Achievements. For achievement conditions, use the official Steam achievement page and verify hidden story conditions in-game before publishing a definitive route.
