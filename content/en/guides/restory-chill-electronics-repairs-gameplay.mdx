ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs is a cozy, narrative-driven electronics repair-shop management simulation by Mandragora and tinyBuild. You receive devices from customers, disassemble and clean them, replace faulty parts, reassemble them, manage money and supplies, and return the finished work. The Y2K-era browser, online orders, customer choices, and multiple endings make the repair loop part of a wider shop story.

## The core repair loop

The central activity is hands-on restoration rather than a simple delivery menu. A customer brings a device, the player takes it apart, cleans the parts, replaces the faulty component, rebuilds the device, and completes the order. The exact input sequence and every device-specific interaction are **待确认**, but the stage order is confirmed by the official store description.

## Devices you can restore

The official material describes nostalgic consoles, handhelds, phones, cameras, music players, and home appliances. ## Shop management and supplies

Repair work takes place inside a small business. The confirmed systems include money management, bills, licenses, tools, store walls, shelves, decorations, and online or offline orders. The full-release announcement also mentions upgradeable tools, new devices, more characters, and store customization.

The Y2K-era web browser is part of the economy because it helps the player hunt for spare parts. Email orders have achievement milestones at the first order, 25 orders, and 100 orders. The exact browser name, order refresh rules, prices, unlock dates, and relationship between Internet settings and difficulty are **待确认**.

## Customers and branching stories

ReStory is not only a technical checklist. Customer conversations and choices create a branching storyline with multiple endings, and the official description says those choices can affect customer lives and the shop. Official examples include deciding whether to report a suspicious discovery on a former gangster’s phone and whether to help a lovestruck student propose.

The total ending count, formal route names, trigger conditions, and post-ending shop state are **待确认**. A gameplay overview can safely promise branching choices and replay value, but it should not publish a route table without full-game verification.

## Customization, competitions, and replay value

Device customization is part of the full-release feature set, and the Demo announcement mentions an airbrush and palettes. Steam Achievements also track painting, stickers, shop comfort, Zen points, and assembly competitions. The complete customization inventory and unlock order are **待确认**.

The official material describes a main story of at least 15 hours, with playtime varying by player. Branching stories, device customization, online requests, and competitions create reasons to revisit the shop, but the supplied research does not establish a fixed post-story mode or a guaranteed number of replay routes.
