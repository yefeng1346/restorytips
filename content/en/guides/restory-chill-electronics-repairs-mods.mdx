## Modding versus configuration

Steam settings, Steam Input mappings, graphics configuration, and true game mods are different things. A configuration change may affect how the client launches or how a controller maps inputs, but it does not prove that the game exposes a modding interface. Likewise, a community guide category does not prove that a file is safe or supported.

The supplied sources do not provide a configuration-file path, supported command line, save-edit format, or way to change repair devices. Do not publish installation steps without an official or stable community source.
