ReStory: Chill Electronics Repairs has 50 Steam Achievements in the full version. The official Steam achievements page publishes the names and public conditions, while the official update feed records an early-unlock issue for How Did I Do It and Frugal. Use the checklist below to track the published targets.

## Early repairs and business milestones

The first repair-focused targets are First fix!, for completing the first repair, and How did I do this?, for using a soldering iron to repair a part. Business progress includes All bills paid!, which requires paying 2 bills, and Bankrupt, which is tied to receiving an overdue-bill warning. The public conditions describe the target; they do not establish the safest order for reaching it.

The money milestones are Business Shark, for earning more than 100,000 yen in one day, and Millionaire!, for earning 1,000,000 yen. Review milestones are First reviews! at 5 reviews, Getting popular! at 25 reviews, and Best shop in Akiba! at 50 reviews. Keep the currency and review values as the public conditions, not as a promise that a particular order always produces the same result.

## Internet, orders, and the marketplace

Master of the Internet is tied to the first email order. WWW requires 25 email orders, and Internet Business requires 100 email orders. These achievements confirm that online work is a trackable part of the shop.

Marketplace progress includes Flipper for buying 5 devices in the marketplace, Garage sale! for buying 25, and Making money! for selling 5 devices. Use those public conditions to track marketplace progress.

## Cleaning and shop equipment

Clean Job is connected to cleaning the workspace. Big cleaning! requires cleaning 100 parts, and Galactic cleaning! requires cleaning 1,000 parts. Hands Free is tied to buying the automatic ultrasonic bath, confirming the bath as part of the formal cleaning system.

Tool and accessory achievements include Twist and turn for buying a professional screwdriver, Shredder for buying a broken-parts shredder, Lootbox for buying a parts box, and Gambler for buying 50 parts boxes. These public conditions track the shop tools and accessory purchases.

## Licenses, customization, and time

Official Partner requires buying any license. Golden Partner requires 5 licenses, Are you serious? This is absurd! is tied to buying the most expensive license, and Platinum Partner requires all licenses.

Customer customization has its own group. Custom orders available! requires painting a device for the first customer order, Instant Cool requires applying 1 sticker, Instant Cool-er requires 25 stickers, and Sticker Bombing requires 100 stickers.

Time and comfort targets are Cozy!, for buying a comfort item, and A Place Of Zen, for exceeding 100 Zen points. Akiba never sleeps! requires 7 days without ending the day; Employee of the Month is tied to day 30, Quarterly Report to day 90, and Akiba Feels Like Home to day 365. These achievements make shop time and comfort part of the long-term checklist.

## Competitions, combined orders, and hacking

Promise of Akiba requires winning 1 assembly competition. Star of Akiba requires winning 3 different device assembly competitions, and Legend of Akiba requires at least one win for each device type.

Jack of all trades requires completing an order that includes painting, cleaning, repair, and hacking. Hacking 101 is tied to the first reprogramming action, while 11001 requires reprogramming 25 devices. These conditions show how different systems can meet in one order, but the exact availability of hacking and the required tools should be recorded from the full release.

## Story and hidden achievements

The public achievement page includes Origami, Melancholy, Ronin, Rock for the ages!, Ghost in the frame, and Globalization as story or hidden achievements. Keep these names separate from the ending guide.
