## A safe troubleshooting record

When a player reports a resolution or frame problem, capture platform, display resolution, window mode, VSync state, target frame rate, and the affected repair action. This turns a vague “settings do not work” report into a reproducible note. It also keeps Steam Deck compatibility discussions separate from Windows or Mac settings.

If the issue is a device stuck during disassembly, link to the Cleaning and Controller pages only when the observed symptom matches their confirmed scope.
